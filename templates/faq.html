Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ShadowHub

Browse our shop, add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, fill in your contact information, select your preferred cryptocurrency, and place the order. You'll then be redirected to the payment instructions page with a wallet address and QR code.

We accept Bitcoin (BTC), Monero (XMR), USDT (TRC20), and TRON (TRX). We recommend Monero for maximum privacy as it is untraceable by design.

Payment verification typically takes 1-2 hours. Bitcoin requires 6 blockchain confirmations (about 60 minutes). Monero and TRON are usually confirmed faster. Once verified, your order will be processed immediately.

We offer refunds if your order is not delivered within the expected timeframe, or if the product is significantly different from the description. Refunds are issued in the same cryptocurrency at the current market rate. Contact our support team with your order number to initiate a refund.

Yes! We collect only the minimum information required to fulfill your order. We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Cryptocurrency payments add another layer of anonymity. For maximum privacy, use Monero (XMR) which is completely untraceable.

Visit the Order Tracking page and enter your order number and email address. You can see the full status history of your order including payment verification, processing, and shipping stages.

If you miss the 30-minute window, please contact our support team immediately with your order number. In most cases, we can extend the payment window. However, the exchange rate may have changed, so the required amount might be different.

Shipping times vary by product type and destination. Digital products are delivered instantly after payment confirmation. Physical products typically ship within 1-3 business days and delivery takes 5-14 days depending on your location.

No account is required to place an order. You can shop anonymously by simply entering your contact information at checkout. Creating an account gives you access to order history and faster checkout in the future.

Use our Contact Form to reach our support team. Please include your order number if your inquiry is about a specific order. We respond within 24-48 hours.
