How to Order

Simple, anonymous, and secure - here's how it works

1
Browse Products

Browse our extensive catalog. Use filters to find exactly what you need. No account required to browse.

2
Add to Cart

Add your selected items to cart. You can adjust quantities or remove items at any time before checkout.

3
Pay with Crypto

Enter your contact info and select your preferred cryptocurrency. Send the exact amount to our wallet.

4

After payment verification, your order will be processed and shipped. Track your order any time.

TRON (TRX) Fast transactions - low fees
Payment Process
  1. Select your items and proceed to checkout.
  2. Enter your contact information and select payment method.
  3. After placing order, you'll receive a wallet address and QR code.
  4. Send the exact USD equivalent in your chosen cryptocurrency.
  5. You have 30 minutes to complete the payment.
  6. Copy your transaction hash (TXID) from your wallet.
  7. Paste the TXID on the confirmation page to verify payment.
  8. We will confirm your payment within 1-2 hours and process your order.

Quick Questions

Payment verification typically takes 1-2 hours depending on blockchain confirmation times. Bitcoin may take up to 6 confirmations. Monero and TRON are usually faster.
