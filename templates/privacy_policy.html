Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2024

1. Information We Collect

ShadowHub is committed to minimal data collection. We only collect information that is strictly necessary to process your orders:

  • Order information: name, email, shipping address (if applicable)
  • Payment information: cryptocurrency transaction hashes (we never see or store private keys)
  • Usage data: anonymized browsing patterns to improve our service

We do NOT collect: government IDs, credit card numbers, social security numbers, or any biometric data.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively for:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders
  • Communicating order status updates to you
  • Resolving disputes and providing customer support
  • Improving our platform and services

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Retention

We retain order data for a minimum period necessary to fulfill legal obligations and resolve disputes. After this period, your data is permanently deleted from our systems. You may request early deletion by contacting our support team.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use only essential cookies required for the website to function correctly (session management, cart storage). We do not use third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or behavioral analytics tools.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Access controls limit who can view customer data

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt-out of any marketing communications

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through our contact form.
